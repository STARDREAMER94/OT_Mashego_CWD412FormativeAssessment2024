About Us

Our Mission

CTU-Buddy is designed to enhance the learning experience at City Technical University by providing students with a comprehensive and interactive educational platform that streamlines academic management, fosters collaborative learning, and promotes resource sharing. Our mission is to support students in managing their schedules, participating in discussions, sharing resources, and connecting with the CTU community. We aim to enhance students’ learning experiences and support their academic success.

CTU-Buddy Vision

At CTU-Buddy, our vision is to revolutionize the student experience at City Technical University by creating a comprehensive digital ecosystem that fosters academic success, collaboration, and personal growth. We aspire to be the go-to platform that seamlessly integrates essential academic tools, resources, and community features into one user-friendly interface.

Our goal is to empower students with the tools they need to manage their educational journey effectively, connect with peers and faculty, and access valuable resources that support their learning and development. By continuously innovating and responding to the needs of our users, we aim to enhance the overall educational experience and contribute to the creation of a vibrant and engaged campus community.

Development History

The concept for CTU-Buddy originated from a shared obstacle experienced by numerous students at City Technical University: juggling hectic schedules, navigating learning materials, and participating in valuable academic conversations in a fragmented digital setting. In 2024, our team of developers, designers, and educators united with a common objective: to transform student engagement with the learning environment by creating a centralized, user-friendly platform.

Throughout a number of months, our team carried out surveys, collected input from students and faculty, and examined the most effective methods to meet these requirements. Following several brainstorming sessions and prototypes, CTU-Buddy started to come together. Our main goal was to develop a user-friendly interface that includes key components like a timetable organizer, discussion boards, and sharing tools, while also guaranteeing compatibility with all devices.

As we progressed through the design, development, and testing stages, we consistently improved CTU-Buddy to ensure a smooth and satisfying user experience. Presently, CTU-Buddy functions as a thorough educational platform created to aid students in all areas of their academic experience, such as organizing schedules and working with classmates. The development process began in mid 2024, with key milestones including:

  • Initial Concept and Planning - July 2024
  • Design and Prototyping - July 2024
  • Development and Testing - August 2024
  • Launch and Feedback Collection - September 2024
  • Ongoing Updates and Improvements - 2024
